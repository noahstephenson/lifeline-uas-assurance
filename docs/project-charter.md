# Project charter

Project Lifeline will demonstrate a traceable mission-assurance architecture for one
simulated multirotor carrying a notional medical package. The system observes link,
navigation, and energy health; recommends a constrained response; makes assumptions and
rejected alternatives visible; and produces verification evidence.

Success is a reproducible fake-source campaign, a live PX4 SITL demonstration, historical
replay, and a claim-evidence package. Real flight, military operations, patient decisions,
certification, and safety claims are outside the boundary.
